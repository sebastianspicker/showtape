# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The primary user is an Apple Music subscriber who wants to turn one concert setlist into an ordered playlist without understanding APIs or matching algorithms. Repeat users need quick access to recognizable recent imports and must be able to correct unusual or live track matches without losing their work. Maintainers need deterministic fixtures and explicit states for support and release verification. People using screen readers, keyboard or switch input, zoom, reduced motion, or low-vision settings must be able to complete the same workflow.

## Product Purpose

Setlist to Playlist converts a public setlist.fm URL or ID into an Apple Music playlist through four explicit stages: import, preview, match, and export. Success means a user can confirm the show, understand every match, safely authorize Apple Music only when needed, recover from partial failures, and finish in a few minutes on a phone or desktop.

## Brand Personality

Focused, calm, and trustworthy. The product speaks plainly, respects the user's attention, and feels like a working setlist: ordered, compact, legible, and direct.

## Anti-references

This is not a glassmorphic SaaS dashboard, an Apple product imitation, a concert-poster landing page, or a generic premium AI interface. Avoid gradients, glows, hover lifts, decorative elevation, cards around every stage, ornamental status color, and vague motivational copy.

## Design Principles

1. Keep one current task and one clear next action.
2. Describe system state truthfully; pending work is never presented as failure or completion.
3. Preserve valid user work when moving backward through the flow.
4. Make external mutations explicit and prevent accidental repetition.
5. Use familiar, compact patterns that disappear into the task.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA and a deliberate 44px minimum for normal controls in this phone-first product. Use semantic HTML, visible focus, explicit text alongside status color, focused step headings, keyboard-complete interaction, 400% reflow, and reduced-motion behavior. Essential text must be at least 14px.
